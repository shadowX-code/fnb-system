# Factory Master Data

## Purpose And Scope

This domain owns Factory Product Recipes/BOM, Production SOP, Factory Location/reference master data, MeSTI Cleaning and Calibration setup configuration, and Factory-owned suppliers and customers.

## Canonical Ownership

Current Factory master-data services, migrations, trusted save authorities, RLS, routes, and tests are authoritative.
Factory Production and Warehouse consume this data but own execution evidence and stock state respectively.
Restaurant suppliers and recipes remain restaurant-owned unless a deliberate shared contract exists.

## Core Entities

- Factory products and raw materials, including Raw Material Acceptance Procedure and Control Methods
- Product Recipe/BOM drafts, versions, components, quantities, units, and yield context
- Production SOP records, versions, attachments, and status
- Factory Locations and other Factory reference/configuration records
- Factory Equipment categories and equipment instances
- MeSTI Cleaning of Area and Cleaning of Equipment Requirements
- MeSTI Calibration Settings, versioned Requirements, Schedule, and immutable Records
- MeSTI Health Declarations for canonical Employees and Factory visitors
- MeSTI Operator Hygiene Inspection evidence for canonical Employees
- MeSTI Waste Disposal requirements and daily Location-centric disposal evidence
- Factory suppliers and customers

## Lifecycle And Business Rules

Product Recipe/BOM Draft saves use the established trusted save authority so header and component changes remain atomic and validated.
Each BOM component persists its `recipe_usage_uom` for audit stability. Raw Material master data is the only authority for a new BOM row: a valid package conversion uses its dimensional Base UOM; otherwise Recipe usage uses Storage UOM. Recipe users edit quantity, not UOM. Existing BOM quantities and usage UOMs remain historical evidence and are never reinterpreted or rewritten by a Raw Material conversion change. Recipe previews may show incomplete cost where no declared path exists, and never invent a package conversion.
For both Product Recipes and Production SOPs, Create establishes only the first Finished Good family. Once any Draft, Active, or Archived version exists, continuation is only through the canonical New Version authority. Trusted saves serialize first-family creation and reject concurrent duplicate family creation without exposing database constraint text. Production SOP Drafts may be created and edited without Equipment bindings so process authoring/import remains possible; Equipment is an incomplete Draft configuration until activation. Activation requires at least one currently active canonical Factory Equipment binding, while only active Equipment may be newly bound.
Every Production SOP version pins one explicit Product Recipe version. An unused Draft SOP may repin to the current active Recipe in place after review, retaining its SOP version, steps, sub-steps, Equipment, and QC configuration. Active, Archived, or Production-referenced SOPs remain recipe-immutable and must use the controlled New Version path. Recipe changes produce a review signal for newly introduced ingredients that have not been referenced by an SOP step; the signal never generates process steps or adds an activation requirement.
Published or execution-ready recipes and SOPs are versioned or pinned according to current contracts.
Later edits must not rewrite the recipe, BOM, or SOP context attached to historical production.

Master-data records may be retired or made inactive according to current state rules, but referenced history remains valid.
Finished Good Tags are reusable descriptive metadata attached to the canonical product family, independent of Category and Packaging SKU. All SKUs inherit their family's stable tag IDs. Rename preserves assignments; archive retains existing references but forbids new assignments. Permission-checked, audited tag and assignment RPCs own writes, while a single RLS-preserving batched snapshot supplies manager, filter, Grouped/Table views and detail. Tags never classify or mutate inventory, Recipe, Production or batch authority.
Raw Material Acceptance Procedure and Control Methods are canonical master-data guidance. Raw Material Receiving reads the current Acceptance Procedure while drafting and snapshots both fields when Receiving is completed, so later master edits cannot rewrite receipt evidence.
Factory Suppliers and Raw Materials have one canonical many-to-many eligibility relationship. A link says an active Supplier may supply an active Raw Material for new Factory Receiving; it does not assign inventory ownership or alter historical Receiving evidence. Supplier link management and all supplier-scoped receiving pickers consume the same eligibility authority. Historical Receiving remains readable even if its Supplier/Raw Material pairing is later unlinked or either master record is retired.
Factory Location is the canonical physical-location master. The legacy internal `factory_storage_locations` contract remains stable for compatibility, while user-facing UI labels it as Location. `is_storage_location` controls whether an active Location may be selected by raw material, receiving, production output, finished goods, and stock workflows. Non-storage active Locations may still be referenced by appropriate non-inventory Factory workflows such as MeSTI Cleaning. Locations may show a read-only current-inventory summary and batch drill-down derived from the canonical positive Raw Material and Finished Good batch balances; this does not create Location-to-product links or movement records. Each inventory family is visible only to roles with its corresponding canonical inventory-view permission, so Location access alone does not disclose stock counts or batch detail.

Factory Equipment is Factory-owned and separate from Restaurant Asset Tracking. Each equipment instance has one canonical current Factory Location, an optional Factory Equipment Category, and a lifecycle status. Active equipment is eligible for new Production SOP bindings; inactive, maintenance, and out-of-service equipment remain readable through immutable production and compliance snapshots.

MeSTI Cleaning of Area Requirements bind tasks directly to one or more canonical Factory Locations. MeSTI Cleaning of Equipment has two canonical sources: scheduled Requirements bind tasks to active Equipment on a daily or selected-weekday recurrence, while After Production occurrences are created only when a completed Production uses a canonical Equipment binding from its pinned Production SOP. After Production occurrences use the Production operational `end_date`, not the audit completion timestamp. After Production is not a configurable Requirement and does not rely on free text, manual actual-equipment entry, recipes, or batch creation. Both workflows preserve immutable occurrence evidence; scheduled Requirements use stable logical identities with forward-effective versions and equivalent saves are no-ops.

MeSTI Cleaning of Area, Cleaning of Equipment, Calibration, and Waste Disposal Requirements use the same effective-date rule. An unchanged save is a no-op. A future current version may be edited in place; after a version is effective, a meaningful edit creates a non-overlapping forward successor and leaves issued/completed/submitted/verified evidence attached to its existing version and date. MeSTI Calibration Requirements bind one active Factory Equipment instance and Calibration Type to one logical, forward-effective version lineage. The server rejects duplicate active Equipment plus Calibration Type pairs. Schedule and Records are trusted RPC projections. Only a verified Pass becomes Last Valid Calibration and advances Next Due; a verified Fail remains permanent evidence, projects as Failed, and never renews validity. A later verified Pass is the only recovery path. Equipment, category, and Location are snapshotted when a record is created so later master-data or requirement changes cannot rewrite history.

Cleaning of Area preserves the explicitly selected Effective From date instead of silently clamping it to today. A new requirement may start in the past. An unissued future version may be corrected in place only without crossing predecessor/version or issued-occurrence boundaries; adjacent empty version intervals are reconciled atomically. Other meaningful edits create a successor strictly after the current version start and every issued occurrence, including Pending occurrences. Unsafe dates return the earliest valid date, and stale requirement versions must be reloaded. Existing occurrence identities, snapshots, dates, statuses and audit evidence are never rewritten by requirement saves.

Cleaning Occurrences are generated/materialized by trusted database authorities and preserve immutable requirement/source snapshots. Area occurrences are unique by logical requirement, Location, and due date. Equipment scheduled occurrences are unique by logical requirement, Equipment, and due date; After Production occurrences are unique by Production and SOP-bound Equipment. Daily is Location- or Equipment-centric as applicable. Equipment Cleaning Monthly is a canonical Equipment-centric projection: one Equipment row aggregates every Scheduled and After Production obligation for each date, retains per-status counts and a monthly summary, and exposes the underlying immutable occurrence/provenance evidence for drill-down.

Area and Equipment Cleaning share one fixed server-owned Cleaning Method catalog: Cleaning, Sanitization and Clean-in-Place. New requirements select exactly one method. Trusted saves derive the supplied materials, ordered reference steps and Visual Inspection reference from that catalog, never from client-authored procedure text. Method changes follow the existing requirement version/effective-date authority; an effective or evidenced method cannot be changed directly in place. Each requirement version and newly issued occurrence freezes its method reference. Daily/Monthly evidence reads that original snapshot, never the latest requirement. Existing requirements and snapshots are not backfilled: absent methods remain Not Specified, including After Production evidence without an explicit method source. An unchanged legacy save remains a no-op; an explicitly selected method cannot be cleared. These are reference procedures, not validated chemical-use parameters: no concentrations, contact times or temperatures are inferred, and no completion checklist or mandatory verification field is added.

Legacy logical requirements whose entire lineage has no method may continue versioning as Not Specified until an explicit method is selected. This compatibility does not permit a new logical requirement without a method or clearing a previously specified method.

Units, quantities, product/material relationships, storage eligibility, recurrence configuration, and duplicate constraints are server-validated where protected.

## Permissions, Versions, And Audit

Admin access requires the relevant Factory master-data permission and applicable scope.
Trusted saves derive actors server-side and use explicit grants.
Recipe/BOM versions, SOP publication/version state, material relationships, MeSTI Cleaning completion/verification evidence, MeSTI Calibration requirement/record/verification evidence, and meaningful supplier/customer/location changes retain auditability.

MeSTI Cleaning of Area, Cleaning of Equipment, Calibration, Operator Hygiene, and Waste Disposal use canonical FeedX permissions as their only authorization source: view read models, create/edit/manage requirement setup, complete recordable work, and review/verify outcomes. Module-specific Responsible/Verifier role mappings do not exist. A user who holds the relevant canonical review/verify permission may verify their own submitted/completed evidence; the server still derives the actor from Admin Auth and rejects every transition without that permission.

MeSTI Health Declaration is one unified Factory compliance evidence domain for Employee and Visitor submissions. Employee declarations reference the canonical Employee master and freeze an identity snapshot so later employee changes cannot rewrite historical evidence; visitor context is declaration-local. Symptoms are structured values and an empty symptom set is the sole No symptoms representation. New declarations use separately identifiable vomiting, sore-throat, skin/open-wound, eye, ear, and nose codes; selecting Other requires a separate symptom detail while general Notes remain optional. Legacy combined ear/nose/eye values remain immutable readable history and are never split or rewritten. The server derives Fit for Work/Cleared versus Health Issue Declared from the symptom set. A permitted manager may correct an existing declaration in place or void it; a void retains its immutable identity, actor, timestamp, and audit entry rather than physically deleting evidence. Retry safety uses a caller request ID scoped to the authenticated recording employee. Canonical Health Declaration view, create, and manage permissions are enforced by trusted RPCs; there are no module-specific role mappings.

MeSTI Operator Hygiene Inspection records one daily inspection session keyed by inspection date. Entries reference canonical active Employees and freeze employee name/position snapshots. Clothing and Hygiene are recorded as Pass/Fail; the server derives Overall as Compliant only when both pass, otherwise Non-Compliant. Non-Compliant entries require both Issue and Action evidence. Draft sessions may be edited through the trusted save RPC; submitted sessions are read-only, and verification is a separate trusted transition authorized only by the canonical verify permission. Daily reads expose inspected/compliant/non-compliant and Submitted/Verified evidence; Monthly is Employee-centric with date-level drill-down and includes only employees with an actual inspection entry in the selected month.

MeSTI Waste Disposal uses versioned Location requirements with a positive required count for each scheduled day. Requirements may recur Daily or on selected Weekly weekdays. A daily disposal session records any number of immutable disposal events, each freezing the canonical Location and server-derived completing employee/time. Requirement edits are no-ops when unchanged; a future current version may be edited in place and an effective requirement creates a forward version. Completion is count-based, so extra events are retained and compliant when completed count meets or exceeds the requirement. Submitted and verified sessions are read-only; whole-day verification is governed by the canonical verify permission. Monthly is Location-centric, exposes the same record/submit/verify evidence lifecycle, and represents unscheduled dates as blank rather than a failure.

## Workflows And Integrations

Authorized Admins maintain products/materials, compose and version recipes/BOM, manage production SOPs, configure Locations, MeSTI Cleaning Requirements and Calibration requirements, and maintain supplier and customer records.
Factory Production pins the appropriate master-data versions for planning and execution.
Factory Warehouse consumes material, product, storage, supplier, and customer references for receipts, movements, and dispatch.
Factory Production consumes canonical Equipment bindings from the pinned Production SOP at completion; free-text SOP instructions remain instructional and are not a trigger. Factory MeSTI Cleaning of Area consumes canonical Locations and canonical permissions while owning its daily/monthly compliance occurrence evidence. Cleaning of Equipment consumes canonical Equipment and completed Production SOP bindings while owning its own idempotent, provenance-preserving occurrence evidence.
Factory UI is composed from four durable presentation foundations: Factory UI Foundation Core; Factory List UI Foundation V1 (Management Lists, History, Evidence Reports, and Setup / Requirements); Operational Daily Foundation V1; and Monthly Matrix Foundation V1. Setup / Requirements is a List Foundation composition with the canonical Create/Edit modal, not a separate visual system. Specialized Production workflows retain their domain composition while inheriting Core primitives. Factory List Foundation tables use Factory Data Surface V1: one restrained 12px, theme-token border and surface containing the header, rows, and pagination, without stacked card shadows or page-local table shells. Factory Status Language V1 uses shared semantic colors and two variants: compact text-only badges for tables, lists, setup, and reports; emphasized badges with exactly one semantic icon for fast-scanning operational states. Status badges are reserved for lifecycle/state, while type/source labels, progress, attention values, and entity text retain their own table-cell primitives.

Cleaning of Area is the presentation pilot for Factory Operational Daily, Monthly Matrix, and Setup / Requirements compositions: shared view tabs, compact date or month controls, and a collapsible Task / Location calendar expose server-owned occurrence statuses. Task cells are read-only aggregates; Location cells open a shared, non-modal calendar action popover that stays open across canonical Complete / Verify transactions and authoritative refreshes. Full evidence remains available through optional View Details. Failed/concurrent transitions refresh current evidence; failed refreshes block stale actions until a read-only retry succeeds. Small task lists expand by default, with sticky identity while dates scroll. Setup lists current requirement versions with up to two neutral Location chips, accessible remaining-Location overflow and Effective From. Edit includes a read-only History timeline loaded on demand from the same canonical requirement lineage and Location bindings under existing RLS; it marks the current version and shows only persisted creation/update metadata. Viewing History neither saves the form nor changes unsaved values, version boundaries or occurrence evidence. Historical versions also remain visible through version-pinned Monthly evidence. Shared requirement controls provide Daily / Weekly selection, conditional weekdays, non-search Status, and explicit Location Select All / Clear. Area, Equipment Cleaning and Waste use the shared native-checkbox weekday chip group; Area Locations and Equipment Cleaning use compact multi-select tiles with selection counts, full-tile selection and keyboard focus. Selected chips/tiles use mint fill, a thin semantic border and selected text styling without check icons; native checked state and visible focus remain intact. Area bulk selection retains linked IDs outside the active list; only explicit Clear removes them. Recurrence and permission authorities remain unchanged.
All Factory MeSTI surfaces reuse those frozen foundations according to their workflow: Cleaning, Operator Hygiene, and Waste Disposal use Operational Daily and Monthly Matrix; Calibration setup and requirements use the List Foundation composition; Raw Material Control and Food Processing Control are read-only evidence reports; Health Declaration is an evidence history with declaration actions in the page header. Factory summary cards are one shared system: standard cards serve dashboards, overview, and list summaries; compact cards retain the same icon, semantic, and theme language at operational density. Session state remains a status badge rather than a summary metric. Rich read-only provenance uses the Core evidence presentation pattern: an entity header, semantic state, grouped sections, compact key/value grid, domain evidence, and verification provenance. Food Processing opens existing canonical Production QC-result evidence on demand for compact preview and detailed evidence; it does not create a MeSTI QC ledger. This is presentation-only and does not create a second MeSTI source of truth.
Factory MeSTI Calibration consumes Factory Equipment and canonical permissions while owning its versioned schedule and immutable calibration evidence.
Factory MeSTI Operator Hygiene consumes canonical Employees and canonical permissions while owning daily session lifecycle evidence.
Factory MeSTI Raw Material Control is a read-only projection of canonical Raw Material standards and completed Raw Material Receiving evidence. It owns no raw-material, receiving, supplier, storage, user, or verification duplicate. Receiving verification is document-level: completion posts stock and freezes item guidance snapshots, then the document awaits a permitted second employee's verification; the receiving actor cannot self-verify and verified evidence is immutable.

## Compatibility And Deferred Scope

### External MeSTI Inspection (Phase 1)

`factory_mesti_inspection` is the machine-only redacted read authority. It consumes the existing Factory dataset and canonical requirement versions, recurrence helper, occurrence snapshots, calibration due-date helper, sessions/events and pinned Production evidence. It introduces no organization/company ownership or Factory tenancy. The retained `factory_mesti_inspection.view` code is the Gateway connection prerequisite; every resource additionally requires its own canonical View Records grant. It grants no resource data, employee identity or Admin session by itself. The reusable machine credential boundary is documented in `../architecture/trusted-authorities.md`.

The Staging-only `factory-mesti-mcp` Edge Function preserves five compatibility review tools: `get_mesti_daily_status`, `list_mesti_exceptions`, `list_pending_verifications`, `get_mesti_evidence_details`, and `get_mesti_requirement_schedule`. It uses authenticated stateless Streamable HTTP with JSON responses and at most 31 Malaysia business dates per request. Business reads use the anonymous client and machine-token authority; a separate server-only client appends bounded Gateway access telemetry. An omitted daily date means yesterday in `Asia/Kuala_Lumpur`. Evidence references are bounded to their embedded operational date; unresolved references fail. No Complete, Verify, Edit, Delete, materialization, AI provider request, or report write-back is performed. The function refuses to run against a non-Staging Supabase URL.

The private `feedx_integrations.mesti_rows` projection never calls UI read RPCs that materialize occurrences. Scheduled Cleaning uses canonical effective versions and Daily/Weekly recurrence, then preserves issued snapshots even after master changes. For past due obligations, no issued occurrence is Missing; a saved pending/missed occurrence is Overdue. Completed evidence awaits its canonical module verification, Unsatisfactory evidence has Evidence Issues, and a re-completed occurrence follows its current Completed state while retaining prior verification provenance. Waste is count-based, preserves extra/unscheduled events, and distinguishes incomplete evidence from submitted/verified sessions. Calibration is evaluated at the end of each requested Malaysia date using the effective requirement version: only verified Pass evidence renews validity, while a later verified Fail remains an issue. Calibration records retain their own completion/verification state separately from schedule validity.

Production-backed checks use `end_date`, never audit completion timestamps. Missing pinned SOP/Equipment or missing after-production occurrences are Unavailable, not fabricated obligations or snapshots. Food Processing matches typed QC points to the exact pinned SOP and persisted QC results; absent legacy point linkage is Unavailable, zero resolvable QC points is No QC Required, and known failed checks remain issues. Finished Product Storage reports canonical batch provenance; it does not infer temperature or storage-condition compliance.

Every response includes all eight modules' daily coverage, data-quality limitations, structured status/severity, evidence reference and recommended human follow-up. Health Declaration exposes only non-voided daily declaration counts, with no identity, health status, symptoms, notes, actions or contact information. Operator Hygiene exposes session-level counts/lifecycle only. Neither module has a canonical dated required-personnel denominator: empty or partial evidence is never classified as full compliance or an invented missing-person finding. Historical master eligibility without an issued snapshot, unresolved requirement setup and undated legacy Production remain explicit limitations. Operational-date evidence uses its current saved state; this is not a complete reconstruction of every historical edit.

Connection, revocation, QA and Grok compatibility limitations are in `../testing/factory-mesti-mcp-staging.md`.

Product Recipes/BOM, Production SOP, Locations, MeSTI Cleaning and Calibration setup, Suppliers, and Customers are grouped here because they are Factory reference authorities.
Historical refactor documents remain deep reference only.
Product lifecycle management, supplier portals, customer CRM, and cross-company master-data federation are deferred unless explicitly introduced.


### External MeSTI Resources

The unified `factory_agent_resource_query` read authority owns Factory resource queries; `factory_mesti_resource_query` is a restricted compatibility adapter for the five original raw MeSTI resources. It returns raw, redacted canonical resource data without severity, compliance verdicts or recommended follow-up. Requirements expose effective versions for Area/Equipment Cleaning, Calibration and Waste, plus scoped typed/legacy SOP QC definitions. Occurrences expose saved Cleaning occurrences, Calibration records, Hygiene/Waste sessions, daily Health counts and completed-Production compliance contexts. Statuses expose only saved lifecycle/verification states, their source and timestamps. No expected occurrence is fabricated to fill a saved-record gap.

Schedules use canonical Daily/Weekly recurrence, exclusive effective-until intervals, issued Cleaning snapshots, current eligible subject bindings where no snapshot exists, the canonical Calibration due-date helper with end-of-Malaysia-date verified Pass evidence, and pinned Production End attribution. Unresolved SOP/equipment bindings return explicit coverage markers without invented subjects or missing occurrences. Food Processing and Storage schedule rows describe Production End compliance contexts, not a new Production authority. Health, Hygiene and Storage have no standalone versioned requirement collection; Health/Hygiene lack a canonical dated required-personnel denominator. These limits remain explicit rather than generating missing/compliant findings.

Redacted Evidence exposes actual cleaning results, calibration result/verification metadata, Hygiene session aggregates, Waste sessions/events, typed or legacy Production QC proof and canonical batch-storage provenance. It never exposes actor/employee identity, individual health state, symptoms, visitors, contacts, notes, free-form remarks or arbitrary snapshots. QC remarks are represented only by presence, and storage proof excludes general inventory quantities. Operational dates are the module’s canonical due/calibrated/inspection/disposal dates or Production End date; health counts use Malaysia declaration dates and exclude voided declarations. Saved states are current observations, not a reconstruction of every historical edit. Resource permissions and cross-resource isolation are described in the trusted-authorities document.

### Gateway MeSTI evidence alignment

Navigation-scoped Cleaning reads include the recorded method and its allowlisted reference procedure from the requirement version or issued occurrence snapshot. They never call Daily/Monthly loaders that materialize occurrences, and never fill legacy missing methods from current setup. Raw Material Control shares its active-standard and completed-receiving report authority with Admin. It excludes drafts/cancelled documents and returns saved verification state plus frozen guidance, with foreign identities separately gated and staff/cost/contact fields removed. The existing QA Executive review scope and credential are unchanged.

### Review resource compatibility

The preserved `mesti_review` resource uses canonical `date` for its advertised `business_date` filter and nested `evidence.record_status` for `record_status`; neither is a compliance-status alias. Its existing finding payload stays unchanged. A shared private `mesti_review_metadata` authority supplies legacy coverage and the resource response's `review_coverage`, `data_quality_limitations`, and `undated_completed_production_count`. Coverage describes the entire authorized date window independently of filters and pagination. Undated completed Productions remain excluded from dated findings; their count is a coverage warning, never an invented dated obligation. Navigation-only module responses do not receive this cross-module metadata. Both source rows and response sizes remain bounded.
