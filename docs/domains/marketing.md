# Marketing

## Scope And Status

Marketing is one native FeedX Admin workspace for organization-wide and brand-scoped marketing. It shares Supabase Auth, canonical employee roles, the Workspace Launcher, module registry, pathname owner and Admin design system. It is separate from Restaurant navigation. Brands are data scope, never independent workspaces.

The implemented foundation has organization/brand setup, explicit memberships and role brand scope, Brand Knowledge, private uploaded media, Facebook/Instagram creative variants, content review/approval, planned timezone-aware schedules with blocked delivery visibility, unified Content Management with List/Month/Week views and saved-record analytics. Meta OAuth, encrypted token handling, account connection controls, durable publishing adapters and social synchronization are implemented on Staging. Actual Meta credentials and test accounts remain externally configured dependencies; no successful delivery is simulated. Unified Inbox internal cases, handling, approved reply/FAQ workflows and gated AI proposals are implemented as Phase 2. Real Messenger inbound, grounded AI suggestion, staff-approved outbound and signed delivery have passed in the authorized Staging test scope. Real Instagram inbound, grounded AI suggestion, staff-approved outbound, signed echo and exact read receipt have also passed. Phase 2 live acceptance is Complete for the authorized Idamans Staging test scope, with staff-send and autonomous replies disabled after acceptance. These test results do not establish general-customer messaging access. AI Creative Studio is deferred to Phase 5; Phase 3A/3B adds shared customers, brand audiences/consent and manual WhatsApp campaigns; Ads and AI Creative Studio remain deferred.

## Ownership

Platform owns `organizations`, `organization_memberships`, `organization_outlets`, `brands` and `brand_outlets`. `outlets` retains operational ownership; `legal_entities` remains People’s legal-employer master and is not a tenant or brand. Setup does not infer brands from outlet names or backfill existing people/outlets into organizations. An authorized manager creates an organization and receives its first membership; further membership and brand/outlet mappings are explicit. One outlet belongs to one organization; brand targeting refers to canonical outlets inside that organization. A brand may target several outlets, and an outlet may be used by more than one brand within that organization.

Marketing owns Knowledge, Content, assets, approvals, connection metadata, publishing jobs, conversations, reply/FAQ/AI proposal lifecycles and its audit/request ledgers. Conversation and AI reply responsibilities remain distinct from Content; future Campaign and Audience responsibilities have separate authorities. These boundaries are not a new customer master. `factory_customers` represents Factory business customers and is not a restaurant member/CRM authority. The approved Phase 3A addition establishes Platform-owned customer/contact identities and explicit brand relationships; no loyalty balance, transaction or redemption authority is created. Anonymous Crew feedback and Factory product-feedback respondents do not establish customer identity or messaging consent.

Finance/Reporting remains the authority for financial calculations and source semantics. Marketing has no financial or loyalty mutation permissions. Future consumers must use authorized owner-provided read contracts and retain outlet permission boundaries, missing evidence and conversion/attribution distinctions. Organization-wide Marketing reads mean the caller’s authorized brands within one organization, not unrestricted whole-database aggregation. This foundation isolates Marketing data; it does not retrofit tenancy into all existing FeedX domains or implement public onboarding/billing.

## Identity And Permissions

Every Marketing RPC derives an active employee through the exact `employees.auth_user_id` link and validates active role, `marketing_workspace.access`, action permission, organization membership and applicable brand scope. Crew sessions cannot enter this authority. Owner/Admin retains canonical role action authority but can see only organizations in which that employee has explicit membership. Other roles default to no brand data until `marketing_role_scopes`/`marketing_role_brands` grants All or Selected Brands. Organization membership alone never grants Marketing action permissions.

Action permissions live in the canonical permission catalog/module registry. Content separates view/create/edit/upload/review/approve/publish/execute/cancel. Publish plans schedules; Execute approves external execution of the exact reviewed revision to enabled accounts. Calendar, Overview, Analytics and Settings have independent view permissions; knowledge management and integration/scope configuration are separate. Marketing permissions do not require Restaurant outlet scope. Changing role brand scope requires both `roles.edit` and `marketing_settings.configure`; it cannot grant beyond the caller’s scope. Shared structure management requires `platform_organizations.manage`, membership of the affected organization, and canonical outlet access for mapping. Adding an existing employee also requires `employees.view` and canonical People employee/outlet visibility; canonical role/Auth assignments are never replaced.

Exposed business tables enable RLS and revoke browser/anonymous direct access. Trusted RPCs are the sole client read/mutation path. The private Storage bucket has scoped read and uploader-bound prepared insert policies; it permits no replacement or deletion of historical media. Private helper functions and credentials are outside browser grants. No token or credential is returned in connection metadata.

## Content Authority

A creative concept has one immutable revision payload containing title, canonical outlet targets and one Facebook and/or one Instagram variant. Each variant records format, caption and pinned ready asset IDs from the same brand. Instagram text-only content is rejected; image/reel needs one compatible asset, carousel needs two to ten images. Caption limits and MIME compatibility are validated server-side. Media dimensions, duration and account-specific publishing restrictions require the actual provider adapter before any external execution; registered MIME and byte size alone do not establish full platform compatibility.

Lifecycle: Draft → Review → Approved → Scheduled → Published, with Rejected, Failed and Cancelled outcomes. Submit requires complete captions/media. Approval binds the exact current revision. A material edit appends a revision, returns to Draft, clears approval and cancels unexecuted jobs. Stale expected revisions fail. Published records cannot be edited; partial/uncertain external delivery blocks material edits and cancellation until reconciliation. Rejection retains its reason in append-only audit evidence.

Content commands use server actor, request UUID and payload fingerprint. An exact retry returns the prior result; changed payload/actor for the same UUID fails. Permission and scope are rechecked before returning a retry. Creation and scheduling therefore do not duplicate records/jobs under retries. State checks and row locks serialize approval/scheduling/edit/cancellation. The schedule pins an approved revision, UTC instant and IANA timezone. Browser conversion rejects DST gaps and overlaps. Schedules create durable Blocked jobs with zero attempts and no provider receipt. Connecting never releases this backlog: external execution requires a separate version-bound approval, pinned account generation and server-enabled test account. A saved plan is not delivered content.

## Publishing And Integration Boundary

`marketing_jobs` retains one job per content revision/channel, attempt/failure counts, lease, due instant, execution approval, connection generation, durable provider checkpoints, bounded error and provider receipt. Service-only claim/guard/finish contracts require current authorizer membership, scope and Publish/Execute permissions, current approved revision, connection capability and unexpired credentials. Every network operation rechecks authority; the Staging worker also requires an exact numeric test-account allowlist. Production external execution remains disabled. Canonical Staging Vault and Cron invoke the independently authenticated worker each minute. Missing Meta configuration produces a blocked health result and no provider call.

Each worker invocation advances one asset validation, upload, container operation, processing check or publication. Intent is checkpointed before external writes; actual returned IDs are checkpointed afterward. Definitive token or permission rejection is a permanent per-channel failure, including Instagram container-status reads; transient read failures retain the container for bounded retry. Errors retry at most five times with backoff; processing is deferred across ticks with a separate bounded poll count. An expired lease resumes only completed checkpoints with no pending write; unresolved intents, lost responses or persistence failure after platform acceptance enter Reconciling and cannot permit automatic resend or material edits. Known receipt IDs are checked through Meta before completion. Unknown receipts require investigation; no caption/time matching claims success. Completion requires a checkpointed provider ID, and repeated matching completion is idempotent. Reconnect cannot replace a connection during leased/uncertain execution.

OAuth uses official Facebook Login for Business authorization-code flow, single-use hashed state bound to employee/org/brand/fixed redirect, and server-side long-lived token exchange with verified app ID, subject, expiry, granted scopes, granular targets and Page tasks. Actor-only short-lived discovery offers verified Page and linked professional Instagram selections. Account-selection retries preserve generation. `marketing_private.credentials` stores AES-GCM ciphertext bound to brand/channel/account; server current/previous keys permit controlled rotation. Browser metadata never contains credentials or ciphertext. Expiry, scope revocation and disconnect stop execution. HMAC-verified Meta deauthorization/data deletion remove credentials and Meta-derived projections; opaque confirmation status reveals no identity. Removal retries are idempotent and historical grants are timestamp-bound so later consent is preserved. FeedX-owned creative revisions remain.

The connection UI distinguishes granted platform permissions, test authorization, allowed formats, independently enabled execution, expiry, sync freshness and worker health. Real credentials, app-role/test-account permissions and Meta App Review are external capabilities; declaring an adapter never establishes those authorizations. Exact Staging endpoints and secret names are in [`marketingMeta.md`](../../qa/staging/marketingMeta.md). No AI, customer messaging, ads or spending authority is enabled.

OAuth discovery emits a bounded, sanitized server trace tagged `marketing_meta_discovery_v1`: recognized granted/declined/expired scopes, granular numeric asset targets, account-list HTTP status/Graph error code/row count, numeric Page and linked Instagram IDs, recognized Page tasks, candidate acceptance or rejection, and final accepted counts. `/me/permissions` is inspected read-only for diagnostics; failure to inspect it never weakens existing grant checks. When discovery is empty, diagnostics compare minimal account fields, the explicit authorized-user accounts endpoint using the same user token; these list probes never adopt accounts or persist probe credentials. If enumeration provides no usable accounts, the adapter directly reads only numeric Page IDs explicitly targeted by the verified `pages_show_list` grant (bounded to fifty). It requires exact returned identity and a nonempty Page access token, and discovers Instagram only through that Page’s linked professional-account field. These direct accounts reuse actor-bound encrypted selection and brand binding, with unchanged granular scope checks. Direct Page reads never infer tasks. A shared resolver may establish them through account enumeration or the official Page roles edge filtered to the verified token authorizer; empty or unavailable evidence remains unverified. No alternative login or caller-supplied asset lookup is introduced. The existing Login Configuration adds only `pages_manage_metadata` for the documented read-only business-assignment fallback. No tokens, codes, secrets, user IDs, names, handles, provider messages or raw responses enter the trace. Zero usable accounts must be diagnosed from provider row counts and candidate decisions before changing permissions or filtering; expected-account identity is compared against returned IDs, never assumed from the consent UI.

The authenticated Settings connection check performs only fixed read-only Graph requests on the existing credential. A service-only diagnostic-material RPC revalidates the actual Supabase Auth-linked employee, organization membership, brand scope and Settings Configure permission, including errored connections. Generation and permission are rechecked before every request. It never changes binding, token, health or execution state. Its response exposes token type/validity/app and account match booleans, recognized scopes, endpoint, HTTP status, numeric Graph code/subcode and recognized permission names extracted from errors; never provider messages, captions, personal subjects or credentials. Full/minimal Facebook post reads and isolated optional fields distinguish authorization failures from unsupported or separately protected expansions. Facebook sync fetches core Page content and shares independently of likes/comments expansions. Missing comment permission skips that optional read; operation-level denials retain posts and mark metrics unavailable, while invalid tokens or changed authority still stop synchronization. A separate authenticated Retry Sync command may recover only a failed Facebook read after fresh retained-token validity/type/app/expiry, exact Page identity and core-post-read verification. Its service-only RPC rechecks actor and generation, clears the read failure and queues synchronization without changing credentials, bindings, grants or any publishing/execution capability. Instagram publishing retains the linked-Page MANAGE/CREATE_CONTENT task requirement documented by Meta’s media_publish endpoint.

The current adapters support Facebook text/images/carousels and Instagram JPEG images/carousels/MP4 Reels. Facebook Reels and Stories remain unavailable. Immutable Storage bytes and conservative MIME/size/image dimension checks precede sending; actual Instagram container processing validates provider restrictions. Library formats outside the publishing subset remain privately previewable. Synchronization retains paged recent post evidence, actual engagement counts and available Instagram reach/views, with observation times and explicit unavailable metrics. Facebook reach/views remain unavailable; group reach is never inferred by summing posts. A mere interface is not a connected provider. Meta App Review is not needed for the local content/approval/scheduling workflow; production network capability is a separate gate. Live customer messaging, ads and spending execution remain unavailable. Inbox internal workflows are described below.

### Task Evidence And External Blocker

Meta v26 defines tasks on relationship edges, not the Page node: [User accounts](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/user/accounts/) adds that User’s assigned tasks; [Page roles](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/page/roles/) adds person-specific tasks, accepts a `uid` filter and excludes people belonging to a business. The shared resolver uses only token-derived subjects, returns no subject/profile data, and preserves per-channel scope/granular-target checks. Diagnostics inspect the same resolver read-only; they do not update saved connections or execution policy.

The business alternatives have different prerequisites: [User assigned pages](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/user/assigned_pages/) addresses a verified business-scoped user and distinguishes actual `tasks` from merely assignable `permitted_tasks`; [Page assigned users](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/page/assigned_users/) requires a MANAGE-capable Page token, `pages_manage_metadata` and a `business` parameter. Business-scoped and app-scoped user IDs must never be equated or matched by names. Current retained credentials are Page tokens; the authorizing User token is not retained. The OAuth permission manifest retains the original seven scopes and adds only `pages_manage_metadata` for this business-assignment read. Configuration IDs continue to own the official Login for Business permission request; no alternate login or token mode is used. When account enumeration and Page roles lack tasks, the fresh verified USER token reads `/{authorizer}/business_users?fields=id,business{id}`. Only this relationship can map the exact authorizer to a business-scoped ID; bounded complete, unambiguous mappings are required. With a Page-targeted metadata grant, `/{page}/assigned_users?business={mapped-business}` reads only `id,tasks`, with bounded complete pagination and exact mapped-user matching. Explicit literal MANAGE/CREATE_CONTENT is required; `permitted_tasks`, names, app/business UI and other assigned users never substitute. BusinessUser API access is limited to businesses that claimed the app (or their children), and provider errors remain unverified rather than triggering extra scopes. Verified mappings are sealed with the account credential for subsequent Page-token revalidation; User access tokens are never retained. Public metadata and diagnostics exclude authorizer/business-user IDs. Fresh binding always leaves external execution disabled; eligibility and consent do not authorize execution.

Unavailable task evidence is not evidence that Meta denied publishing. The operational publishing flag remains false while verification is incomplete. Empty User-account enumeration plus an absent authorizer on Page roles cannot prove missing access: Meta’s business-user exclusion is a documented limitation, not evidence of an identity match or the cause of a particular empty response. Publishing stays disabled until a supported task-bearing response establishes the actual authorizer’s content task on the exact linked Page. An earlier empty accounts edge did not establish denial; the approved fresh authorization subsequently returned the exact Page and content tasks.



### Publishing acceptance qualification

Exact OAuth-authorizer Business User Mapping is a FeedX verification mechanism for the business-assignment fallback, **not a universal Meta publishing prerequisite**. The provider requirement is the actual token authorizer’s task authority on the exact Page: Facebook’s [v26 Page Feed publishing reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/v26.0/page/feed/#publish) requires CREATE_CONTENT, a Page token and `pages_manage_posts`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_show_list`; Instagram’s [media_publish reference](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/media_publish) requires MANAGE or CREATE_CONTENT on the linked Page. The Page roles edge excludes business users. Identity mapping is needed to attribute a business-assignment row to the exact authorizer rather than another staff member. There is no evidence that missing rows mean Meta denied publishing.

A fixed, read-only diagnostic also attempts `GET /{exact-page}?fields=id,business{id}` using the retained verified PAGE credential when tasks and mapping are unavailable and metadata permission is present. Only an exact Page identity and numeric provider-returned business ID permit `GET /{page}/assigned_users?business={verified-business}&fields=tasks&limit=1`. No UI/business-name input, other assigned user, `permitted_tasks`, posting signal or generic successful read is adopted as exact-authorizer authority. This visibility probe returns sanitized HTTP/Graph status and count only; it does not change capabilities, credentials or execution. Current approved authorization satisfies the business-context permission; exact business assignment is verified through the mapped Business User’s reverse assigned_pages relationship. Earlier provider errors and their corrections remain historical QA evidence.

Instagram additionally documents `ads_management` **or** `ads_read` when the app user received the Page role through Business Manager. Exact-authorizer `assigned_pages` evidence established that assignment, and the explicitly approved `ads_read` grant satisfies this conditional requirement without granting `ads_management`. Page Publishing Authorization and two-factor requirements, when imposed by Meta, remain provider prerequisites. Token validity, scopes, `can_post`, quota and read sync alone do not establish publishing eligibility.

Ordinary Marketing staff use canonical FeedX Auth, organization membership, brand scope and Publish/Execute permissions with the brand’s server-held credential; they do not each need to perform Meta OAuth. Meta task authority remains attached to the credential’s original authorizer. The approved Staging acceptance verified this staff workflow, scheduled execution and per-channel receipt reconciliation. Both connection execution flags are disabled after acceptance. Any later execution still requires an enabled eligible connection and explicit authorization of the exact approved revision and account generations.

## Unified Inbox & AI Chatbot (Phase 2)

`/marketing/inbox` belongs to the existing Marketing workspace and shell. Organization-wide reads include only authorized brands; creation, configuration and conversation authority are brand-scoped. The permission catalog and role editor expose independent `marketing_inbox.view/manage/reply/approve/configure/ai` actions. No existing role grants, Meta scopes or account bindings are changed automatically.

Marketing owns conversations, message evidence, assignment, status, priority, tags, internal notes, immutable reply drafts, FAQ plans and AI proposals. Social participant keys remain private and connection-scoped; Page/Instagram identities never become canonical customer/member records. Internal cases have no external recipient, and notes never masquerade as customer messages. History is paged; the UI labels the latest 50 reply/AI proposals and the bounded 100-FAQ library. Canonical RPCs enforce active employee identity, organization/brand scope and action permission. Tables deny direct authenticated/anonymous access with RLS and revoked grants; public definer RPCs are intentional scoped business authorities, not general CRUD endpoints.

Replies use Draft → Review → Approved/Rejected or Cancelled. Bodies are immutable; revision means a fresh draft. The consumed conversation version and any approved knowledge revision must remain current at review. Approval records a **blocked-only outbox** with no provider message ID. There is no send route, executable outbox state, automatic messaging worker or delivery success simulation. Changes to history invalidate eligibility of earlier drafts even when the historical approval remains visible.

Brand Knowledge needs explicit Inbox approval of an exact current revision; only non-empty `verified_brand_fact` fields enter its immutable snapshot. External research and generated suggestions are excluded. FAQ answers cite approved fact keys, require revision-bound human approval and return to draft after material edits. Sensitive complaint/refund/allergen/food-safety FAQs cannot be approved for automatic handling. EN/ZH/BM risk detection conservatively escalates matching notes/inbound messages, raises priority and retains human takeover. Human resolution requires a recorded note; uncertainty/unmatched automation routes back to a person. Keyword detection assists triage and is not a food-safety or clinical determination.

Brand FAQ modes Off/Suggest/FAQ (Suggest is the default for an unconfigured brand) and human takeover are implemented as internal matching/preview plans. Exact normalized matching uses only current approved FAQs; it never infers an uncertain answer. Eligible verified inbound events may prepare a cited draft, but it still requires human review and cannot send. Turning off takeover never overrides escalation or channel authority. The conservative standard window is 24 hours from the latest verified inbound message; echoes, delivery/read receipts, notes and out-of-order events cannot extend it. No Human Agent tags, ad-window extensions, broadcasts or unsolicited outbound initiation are enabled.

The specialized reply/summary/FAQ adapter reuses the FeedX server Responses API pattern and existing `OPENAI_API_KEY`, while requiring explicit `MARKETING_AI_ENABLED=true`, `MARKETING_AI_MODEL`, brand AI consent and `marketing_inbox.ai`. It sends bounded history with email/phone patterns removed and approved facts; social identity keys, employee identities and credentials are excluded. Redaction is limited pattern removal, not a claim of full anonymization. History is untrusted data, structured output requires valid fact references and sensitive/uncertain answers trigger takeover. Reply suggestions, conversation summaries and FAQ improvements remain proposed until human approval; approved replies become drafts and approved FAQ improvements become FAQ drafts with their own approval. Summary evidence identifies the conversation version and becomes stale after history changes. Requests are claimed once, are limited to 20 per employee/day and do not retry uncertain provider calls automatically. Actual model/token usage is retained by organization/brand, including completed calls with obsolete evidence; costs remain unavailable without a pricing authority. Provider-unavailable UI never displays fabricated suggestions or implies a connected model.
