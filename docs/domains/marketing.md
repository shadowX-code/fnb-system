# Marketing

## Scope And Status

Marketing is one native FeedX Admin workspace for organization-wide and brand-scoped marketing. It shares Supabase Auth, canonical employee roles, the Workspace Launcher, module registry, pathname owner and Admin design system. It is separate from Restaurant navigation. Brands are data scope, never independent workspaces.

The implemented foundation has organization/brand setup, explicit memberships and role brand scope, Brand Knowledge, private uploaded media, Facebook/Instagram creative variants, content review/approval, planned timezone-aware schedules with blocked delivery visibility, monthly/weekly calendar and saved-record analytics. Meta OAuth, encrypted token handling, account connection controls, durable publishing adapters and social synchronization are implemented on Staging. Actual Meta credentials and test accounts remain externally configured dependencies; no successful delivery is simulated. AI Creative Director, Inbox, Audience/WhatsApp and Ads remain later phases.

## Ownership

Platform owns `organizations`, `organization_memberships`, `organization_outlets`, `brands` and `brand_outlets`. `outlets` retains operational ownership; `legal_entities` remains People’s legal-employer master and is not a tenant or brand. Setup does not infer brands from outlet names or backfill existing people/outlets into organizations. An authorized manager creates an organization and receives its first membership; further membership and brand/outlet mappings are explicit. One outlet belongs to one organization; brand targeting refers to canonical outlets inside that organization. A brand may target several outlets, and an outlet may be used by more than one brand within that organization.

Marketing owns Knowledge, Content, assets, approvals, connection metadata, publishing jobs and its audit/request ledgers. Future Campaign, Conversation, Audience and AI responsibilities remain distinct from Content. These boundaries are not a new customer master. `factory_customers` represents Factory business customers and is not a restaurant member/CRM authority. No restaurant customer/member or loyalty balance authority was established during discovery; Phase 4 must resolve its integration before audience implementation. Anonymous Crew feedback and Factory product-feedback respondents do not establish customer identity or messaging consent.

Finance/Reporting remains the authority for financial calculations and source semantics. Marketing has no financial or loyalty mutation permissions. Future consumers must use authorized owner-provided read contracts and retain outlet permission boundaries, missing evidence and conversion/attribution distinctions. Organization-wide Marketing reads mean the caller’s authorized brands within one organization, not unrestricted whole-database aggregation. This foundation isolates Marketing data; it does not retrofit tenancy into all existing FeedX domains or implement public onboarding/billing.

## Identity And Permissions

Every Marketing RPC derives an active employee through the exact `employees.auth_user_id` link and validates active role, `marketing_workspace.access`, action permission, organization membership and applicable brand scope. Crew sessions cannot enter this authority. Owner/Admin retains canonical role action authority but can see only organizations in which that employee has explicit membership. Other roles default to no brand data until `marketing_role_scopes`/`marketing_role_brands` grants All or Selected Brands. Organization membership alone never grants Marketing action permissions.

Action permissions live in the canonical permission catalog/module registry. Content separates view/create/edit/upload/review/approve/publish/execute/cancel. Publish plans schedules; Execute approves external execution of the exact reviewed revision to enabled accounts. Calendar, Overview, Analytics and Settings have independent view permissions; knowledge management and integration/scope configuration are separate. Marketing permissions do not require Restaurant outlet scope. Changing role brand scope requires both `roles.edit` and `marketing_settings.configure`; it cannot grant beyond the caller’s scope. Shared structure management requires `platform_organizations.manage`, membership of the affected organization, and canonical outlet access for mapping. Adding an existing employee also requires `employees.view` and canonical People employee/outlet visibility; canonical role/Auth assignments are never replaced.

Exposed business tables enable RLS and revoke browser/anonymous direct access. Trusted RPCs are the sole client read/mutation path. The private Storage bucket has scoped read and uploader-bound prepared insert policies; it permits no replacement or deletion of historical media. Private helper functions and credentials are outside browser grants. No token or credential is returned in connection metadata.

## Content Authority

A creative concept has one immutable revision payload containing title, canonical outlet targets and one Facebook and/or one Instagram variant. Each variant records format, caption and pinned ready asset IDs from the same brand. Instagram text-only content is rejected; image/reel needs one compatible asset, carousel needs two to ten images. Caption limits and MIME compatibility are validated server-side. Media dimensions, duration and account-specific publishing restrictions require the actual provider adapter before any external execution; registered MIME and byte size alone do not establish full platform compatibility.

Lifecycle: Draft → Review → Approved → Scheduled → Published, with Rejected, Failed and Cancelled outcomes. Submit requires complete captions/media. Approval binds the exact current revision. A material edit appends a revision, returns to Draft, clears approval and cancels unexecuted jobs. Stale expected revisions fail. Published records cannot be edited; partial/uncertain external delivery blocks material edits and cancellation until reconciliation. Rejection retains its reason in append-only audit evidence.

Content commands use server actor, request UUID and payload fingerprint. An exact retry returns the prior result; changed payload/actor for the same UUID fails. Permission and scope are rechecked before returning a retry. Creation and scheduling therefore do not duplicate records/jobs under retries. State checks and row locks serialize approval/scheduling/edit/cancellation. The schedule pins an approved revision, UTC instant and IANA timezone. Browser conversion rejects DST gaps and overlaps. Schedules create durable Blocked jobs with zero attempts and no provider receipt. Connecting never releases this backlog: external execution requires a separate version-bound approval, pinned account generation and server-enabled test account. A saved plan is not delivered content.

## Publishing And Integration Boundary

`marketing_jobs` retains one job per content revision/channel, attempt/failure counts, lease, due instant, execution approval, connection generation, durable provider checkpoints, bounded error and provider receipt. Service-only claim/guard/finish contracts require current authorizer membership, scope and Publish/Execute permissions, current approved revision, connection capability and unexpired credentials. Every network operation rechecks authority; the Staging worker also requires an exact numeric test-account allowlist. Production external execution remains disabled. Canonical Staging Vault and Cron invoke the independently authenticated worker each minute. Missing Meta configuration produces a blocked health result and no provider call.

Each worker invocation advances one asset validation, upload, container operation, processing check or publication. Intent is checkpointed before external writes; actual returned IDs are checkpointed afterward. Errors retry at most five times with backoff; processing is deferred across ticks with a separate bounded poll count. An expired lease resumes only completed checkpoints with no pending write; unresolved intents, lost responses or persistence failure after platform acceptance enter Reconciling and cannot permit automatic resend or material edits. Known receipt IDs are checked through Meta before completion. Unknown receipts require investigation; no caption/time matching claims success. Completion requires a checkpointed provider ID, and repeated matching completion is idempotent. Reconnect cannot replace a connection during leased/uncertain execution.

OAuth uses official Facebook Login for Business authorization-code flow, single-use hashed state bound to employee/org/brand/fixed redirect, and server-side long-lived token exchange with verified app ID, subject, expiry, granted scopes, granular targets and Page tasks. Actor-only short-lived discovery offers verified Page and linked professional Instagram selections. Account-selection retries preserve generation. `marketing_private.credentials` stores AES-GCM ciphertext bound to brand/channel/account; server current/previous keys permit controlled rotation. Browser metadata never contains credentials or ciphertext. Expiry, scope revocation and disconnect stop execution. HMAC-verified Meta deauthorization/data deletion remove credentials and Meta-derived projections; opaque confirmation status reveals no identity. Removal retries are idempotent and historical grants are timestamp-bound so later consent is preserved. FeedX-owned creative revisions remain.

The connection UI distinguishes granted platform permissions, test authorization, allowed formats, independently enabled execution, expiry, sync freshness and worker health. Real credentials, app-role/test-account permissions and Meta App Review are external capabilities; declaring an adapter never establishes those authorizations. Exact Staging endpoints and secret names are in [`marketingMeta.md`](../../qa/staging/marketingMeta.md). No AI, customer messaging, ads or spending authority is enabled.

OAuth discovery emits a bounded, sanitized server trace tagged `marketing_meta_discovery_v1`: recognized granted/declined/expired scopes, granular numeric asset targets, account-list HTTP status/Graph error code/row count, numeric Page and linked Instagram IDs, recognized Page tasks, candidate acceptance or rejection, and final accepted counts. `/me/permissions` is inspected read-only for diagnostics; failure to inspect it never weakens existing grant checks. When discovery is empty, diagnostics compare minimal account fields, the explicit authorized-user accounts endpoint using the same user token; these list probes never adopt accounts or persist probe credentials. If enumeration provides no usable accounts, the adapter directly reads only numeric Page IDs explicitly targeted by the verified `pages_show_list` grant (bounded to fifty). It requires exact returned identity and a nonempty Page access token, and discovers Instagram only through that Page’s linked professional-account field. These direct accounts reuse actor-bound encrypted selection and brand binding, with unchanged granular scope checks. Direct reads never infer Page tasks: publishing remains unavailable until tasks are established through account enumeration. No alternative login, extra scope or caller-supplied asset lookup is introduced. No tokens, codes, secrets, user IDs, names, handles, provider messages or raw responses enter the trace. Zero usable accounts must be diagnosed from provider row counts and candidate decisions before changing permissions or filtering; expected-account identity is compared against returned IDs, never assumed from the consent UI.

The authenticated Settings connection check performs only fixed read-only Graph requests on the existing credential. A service-only diagnostic-material RPC revalidates the actual Supabase Auth-linked employee, organization membership, brand scope and Settings Configure permission, including errored connections. Generation and permission are rechecked before every request. It never changes binding, token, health or execution state. Its response exposes token type/validity/app and account match booleans, recognized scopes, endpoint, HTTP status, numeric Graph code/subcode and recognized permission names extracted from errors; never provider messages, captions, personal subjects or credentials. Full/minimal Facebook post reads and isolated optional fields distinguish authorization failures from unsupported or separately protected expansions. Facebook sync fetches core Page content and shares independently of likes/comments expansions. Missing comment permission skips that optional read; operation-level denials retain posts and mark metrics unavailable, while invalid tokens or changed authority still stop synchronization. A separate authenticated Retry Sync command may recover only a failed Facebook read after fresh retained-token validity/type/app/expiry, exact Page identity and core-post-read verification. Its service-only RPC rechecks actor and generation, clears the read failure and queues synchronization without changing credentials, bindings, grants or any publishing/execution capability. Instagram publishing retains the linked-Page MANAGE/CREATE_CONTENT task requirement documented by Meta’s media_publish endpoint.

The current adapters support Facebook text/images/carousels and Instagram JPEG images/carousels/MP4 Reels. Facebook Reels and Stories remain unavailable. Immutable Storage bytes and conservative MIME/size/image dimension checks precede sending; actual Instagram container processing validates provider restrictions. Library formats outside the publishing subset remain privately previewable. Synchronization retains paged recent post evidence, actual engagement counts and available Instagram reach/views, with observation times and explicit unavailable metrics. Facebook reach/views remain unavailable; group reach is never inferred by summing posts. A mere interface is not a connected provider. Meta App Review is not needed for the local content/approval/scheduling workflow; production network capability is a separate gate. No customer messages, ads or spending actions are implemented.

## Knowledge And Evidence

Knowledge saves use expected revision protection and preserve the submitted profile in audit history. Each field distinguishes `verified_brand_fact` (explicit human brand assertion), referenced `external_research` and unverified `ai_suggestion`. External research requires an HTTPS source; UI retains the observation time. No research or AI content is fabricated by the foundation.

Overview/Analytics counts are saved FeedX workflow facts. Social metrics use only saved platform observations; missing values remain unavailable rather than zero. Reach is platform-estimated and cannot be summed into unique cross-brand reach. Conversions, modeled attribution and provider costs remain unavailable. No AI insight or incremental-revenue claim is shown without supporting evidence. Reads are bounded/paged; calendar delivery states are attached to the displayed content page without a global first-100 truncation. Role scope configuration cannot expose or overwrite existing grants outside the manager’s brands. Calendar requests cover at most 42 days and use the declared view timezone. Scope/session changes discard obsolete responses.

## Verification And Phase Boundary

This is L3 work: route/module regressions, timezone and capability tests, production build, SQL grant/scope/lifecycle rehearsal and authenticated canonical Staging UI verification are required. `qa/staging/marketingFoundation.rollback.sql` uses disposable identities/records inside one rollback transaction and calls no provider. Verification evidence and Phase 0 ownership mapping are recorded under `qa/staging/marketingFoundation.md`; Meta setup and contract verification are under `qa/staging/marketingMeta.md` and its rollback rehearsal.

Before Phase 2, complete Phase 1 verification and resolve actual Meta OAuth/worker configuration where external execution is requested. Phase 2 scope is specialized AI planning/creative orchestration, evidence-retaining permitted research, multilingual drafts/scripts/storyboards and human-approved transfer into the same Content revision authority. Existing Recruitment/Crew AI credentials or Guest AI prototype endpoints do not imply authorization or a shared Marketing provider. Inbox, consent/CRM, WhatsApp and Ads remain gated to their later phases.
