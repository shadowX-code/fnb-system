## Scope, authority and freshness

Referenced READ identities are resolved against the existing server-returned capability catalogue. Labels, scope, permission and current AI policy come from those definitions; no copied permission registry is introduced. Every invocation still rechecks human authority, connection policy and source scope. Admin module visibility alone does not authorize a related read. Shared references do not alter tool discovery, dispatch or counts.

The shared read contract returns source, requested scope, retrieval `as_of`, contract version and bounded projection. Retrieval time is not a guarantee that source events are current. Assignment uses its effective date; operational financials and records retain requested periods/completeness; job summaries are all-time; SOPs return current published versions and dates. Paged/limited records cannot be promoted to complete totals. No saved conversation figures or fresh business queries were used to claim coverage.
