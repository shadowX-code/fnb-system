# FeedX retention and deletion proposal

Prepared 2026-10-10 for operator/legal approval. **This is a proposed schedule, not a claim that these timers already run.** Current authorities, migrations and verified Staging behavior govern deletion. Owners must approve triggers, lawful bases and deployment before scheduled purges are introduced. A verified Meta/user deletion request overrides ordinary operational periods; preserve only documented, necessary legal evidence in restricted custody.

## Legal anchors and decisions

- [SSM Companies Act 2016 official reprint](https://www.ssm.com.my/Pages/Legal_Framework/Document/Companies%20Act%202016_Akta%20777_BI%20(1.8.2022).pdf), s245(3), read with current amendments. Seven-year trigger is completion of the relevant transaction/operation.
- [JTKSM Employment Act 1955 official text](https://jtksm.mohr.gov.my/sites/default/files/2023-11/Akta%20Kerja%201955%20%28Akta%20265%29.pdf), s61(2). Legal confirmation must identify the employer, territorial scope and register particulars; this is not a blanket medical/GPS retention mandate.
- [HASiL 2026 employer guidance](https://www.hasil.gov.my/media/3bvlkbww/navigasi-hasil-2026.pdf) specifies seven-year records. [HASiL filing/record FAQ](https://www.hasil.gov.my/individu/soalan-lazim-individu/) illustrates a filing-year trigger. Finance/legal must apply the correct Income Tax Act s82/s82A rule and late-filing exceptions to each business record, rather than applying an individual FAQ universally.

No legal hold is created by default. The privacy operator must document statutory record/court order/legal claim, proof reference, minimum necessary scope, custodian and finite review date. A review date does not silently extinguish a continuing legal obligation. Releasing an archived hold erases its restricted copy through the canonical service function and records a proof hash.

## Processor and restore procedure

1. Inventory Supabase database/Storage, backups/PITR/replicas/export copies; Vercel logs/drains; OpenAI request data; Meta originals. Confirm written processing arrangements, regions, transfer basis and responsible contacts. Marketing does not retain OpenAI response application state; abuse/security copies can still exist under provider rules.
2. Verify the requester's identity/control of the specific account/conversation and responsible brand. Do not equate an OAuth administrator with a social message sender. Record the decision in a restricted privacy case system; FeedX retains only its verification hash/reference.
3. Run the canonical signed callback or service-only verified erasure function. Record its local outcome and exceptions. Do not use ad hoc deletes or wipe an entire brand's independent Brand Knowledge/financial/customer authority.
4. For OpenAI, verify applicable retention/account settings and expiry or obtain a provider deletion outcome where supported. There is no general API for deleting abuse-monitoring copies of `store:false` requests; do not invent one. In-flight requests and legally retained provider records need explicit follow-up. Meta's independently held content requires its own deletion process if requested; this task does not delete external posts/messages.
5. Export the protected erasure journal to an approved restricted recovery location after each case. Following a restore, keep the environment quarantined, import the surviving journal, reapply scoped erasure/tombstones and revocations, verify no operational resurrection, then reopen access. Independent encrypted write-ahead journaling and a disposable logical restore replay are now implemented and verified on Staging; see [privacy operations](feedx-privacy-operations.md). Actual shared-project recovery still requires infrastructure quarantine, key custody and operator verification before reopening; no physical project restore was performed.
6. Verify snapshot/export expiry and processor outcome; use `marketing_privacy_complete_followup` with evidence hash. Pending is the default, with no invented expiry date. A legal archive keeps the case `retained_legal_review`; ordinary staff cannot access it. Public Meta receipt discloses status/timestamps only.

[Supabase backup documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups) distinguishes plan-dependent daily/PITR windows and excludes Storage object binaries from database backups. [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) distinguish application-state and abuse-monitoring retention. Provider rules are not proof of the configured FeedX plan or completion of an individual request.
