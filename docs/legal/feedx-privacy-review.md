## Decisions and operational prerequisites

1. **DPO assessment — likely required; legal decision outstanding:** the Commissioner's [current FAQ](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/faq/) provides three independent tests: over 20,000 subjects, over 10,000 subjects' sensitive/financial information, or regular/systematic monitoring. FeedX's enabled architecture includes recurrent attendance clock/location evidence, rosters, monthly performance/scoring, team reviews and reward evidence; this is a strong basis for the third test even without high volumes. Customer conversation/engagement analytics adds potential monitoring, but live Inbox is not claimed. Treat appointment as likely applicable, not optional merely because FeedX is internal. Legal counsel must confirm actual enabled operations, per-entity controller/processor roles, subject counts and monitoring scope, then document the conclusion. If required, arrange a qualified/conflict-free DPO and notify/register within 21 days of appointment. No DPO is appointed or registration initiated; the supplied privacy contact remains an inquiry channel. See the [official DPO manual](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Manual_Pengguna_Pendaftaran_DPO_EN.pdf).
2. **Controller registration — not established by software operation; legal classification outstanding:** compare each actual controller's activities with the [13 prescribed classes](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/relevant-documents/) and [2026 registration circular](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/personal-data-protection-commissioners-circular-no-1-2026-registration-of-data-controllers/). The Service class is not every service business: it includes specified legal/audit/accounting/engineering/architecture, wholesale/retail and private employment agencies. Ordinary F&B restaurant management is not automatically registered tourist accommodation; software operation is not automatically a telecommunications licensee or licensed direct selling. However the operator's name does not prove whether it actually conducts prescribed accounting/audit, retail or another activity. Legal/operator must confirm SSM business activities, relevant licenses, any applicable wholesale/retail classification and each business's controller role. No exemption is asserted. Registration and DPO tests are separate; PDPA duties still apply outside a prescribed class.
3. **Retention proposal:** [category-specific schedule](feedx-retention-proposal.md) separates statutory register/accounting/tax anchors from proposed operational windows and includes GPS/medical/recruitment, messages, AI, webhooks, logs, content, consent and backups/processors. Approve category triggers, minimum necessary records and responsibility before implementing purge timers. The public copy makes no promise that these proposed periods are already enforced.
4. **Inbox erasure implementation and residual dependencies:** local canonical erasure, immutable grant lineage, derived FAQ provenance, retry tombstones, pre-storage webhook replay protection and sanitized outcome tracking are implemented. Legal holds are removed from operational Inbox and kept in a private archive until reviewed/released. Backup/processor follow-up defaults to Pending and requires evidence; no provider deletion or account reset is simulated. Remaining operational prerequisites are verified backup/export/replica inventory/expiry, protected journal outside restorable snapshots and restore rehearsal, processor retention/deletion confirmation and named privacy operator. These are implementation/operations dependencies, not missing OAuth permissions. Do not activate live messaging or claim fully completed deletion until resolved.
5. **Processors and transfers:** confirm applicable Supabase/Vercel/OpenAI service regions, written processing arrangements, subprocessors, account data settings and Malaysian transfer assessment/safeguards. Meta's [Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms/) sections 3–5 impose separate provider, retention and deletion duties; policy disclosure alone does not satisfy them. [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) distinguish application state from abuse-monitoring retention.
6. **Privacy operations:** confirm the supplied inbox is monitored, authority/identity verification, request handling, escalation, lawful sensitive-data processing and breach-response responsibilities. Do not invent response deadlines or a registered DPO. [Act A1727](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Act-A1727.pdf) covers amended controller/processor, breach and portability duties.
7. **Approval/release:** approve both languages, effective date and disclosures; authorize only the isolated public-page Production release if desired. Verify final canonical HTTPS URL without cookies/auth and with full static text, then request separate approval before saving Meta metadata or publishing the Meta app. Staging visibility is not approval of legal text or external app publication.
