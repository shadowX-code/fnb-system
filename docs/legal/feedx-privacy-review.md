## Decisions and operational prerequisites

1. **DPO assessment:** the Commissioner's current [official FAQ](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/faq/) requires appointment for more than 20,000 individuals, more than 10,000 individuals' sensitive/financial data, or regular/systematic monitoring. The third test is independent of volume; attendance/performance/customer analytics must be assessed, not assumed exempt. If required, appoint a qualified DPO and notify/register with the Commissioner within 21 days of appointment. No appointment or registration has been initiated. DPO notification and data-controller registration are distinct.
2. **Controller registration and roles:** assess actual operator/business activities against the prescribed classes and [2026 registration circular](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/personal-data-protection-commissioners-circular-no-1-2026-registration-of-data-controllers/). Confirm controller/processor arrangements with each employer/customer business; FeedX brand/org labels do not establish the legal relationship. No registration exemption or certificate is claimed.
3. **Retention:** approve a category-based schedule for employee/payroll/legal evidence, messages/media, AI records, webhooks, logs and backups. Verify deletion procedures and restore handling. No invented fixed duration, purge job, indefinite-retention exemption or promise of automatic erasure is used.
4. **Inbox deletion:** before live messaging activation, verify end-to-end deletion of Meta-derived Inbox/AI/provider copies through a governed request process or extend the owning deletion authority with focused QA. Existing callback completion currently covers its older implemented scope only. A public email makes requests accessible but does not prove execution of deletion. User/Meta deletion cannot be denied merely to preserve operational history; legally required retention must be documented. This finding does not authorize an unreviewed destructive deletion.
5. **Processors and transfers:** confirm applicable Supabase/Vercel/OpenAI service regions, written processing arrangements, subprocessors, account data settings and Malaysian transfer assessment/safeguards. Meta's [Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms/) sections 3–5 impose separate provider, retention and deletion duties; policy disclosure alone does not satisfy them. [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) distinguish application state from abuse-monitoring retention.
6. **Privacy operations:** confirm the supplied inbox is monitored, authority/identity verification, request handling, escalation, lawful sensitive-data processing and breach-response responsibilities. Do not invent response deadlines or a registered DPO. [Act A1727](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Act-A1727.pdf) covers amended controller/processor, breach and portability duties.
7. **Approval/release:** approve both languages, effective date and disclosures; authorize only the isolated public-page Production release if desired. Verify final canonical HTTPS URL without cookies/auth and with full static text, then request separate approval before saving Meta metadata or publishing the Meta app. Staging visibility is not approval of legal text or external app publication.
