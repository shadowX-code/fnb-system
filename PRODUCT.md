# Product

## Users

FeedX is used by factory operators, supervisors, managers, warehouse staff, and authorized administrative users working in day-to-day food production and inventory operations.

## Product Purpose

FeedX coordinates factory planning, production execution, quality control, warehouse activity, and traceability. Success means users can scan current operational state quickly, take the correct permitted action confidently, and trust that displayed quantities and lifecycle states match authoritative data.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, work-focused, efficient, and modern. The interface prioritizes operational clarity, dependable interaction, and low cognitive load over decorative styling.

## Anti-references

- Native-looking browser controls where a consistent FeedX control exists
- Grey system dropdowns and native date-picker popups
- Oversized card grids when compact tables or lists scan better
- Decorative dashboard styling that reduces information clarity
- Inconsistent component patterns, weak hierarchy, and unclear status or action states
- Interaction, focus, contrast, or keyboard behavior below WCAG AA

## Design Principles

1. Make authoritative operational state immediately legible.
2. Optimize repeated workflows for fast scanning and low cognitive load.
3. Use one dependable component vocabulary throughout the product.
4. Keep lifecycle states, permissions, and available actions explicit.
5. Prefer dense, structured information over decorative presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard interaction, and status communication. Interfaces must remain usable on desktop and mobile without relying on color alone.
