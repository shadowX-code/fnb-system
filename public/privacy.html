1. Scope and responsibility

The Y Advisory Sdn Bhd operates FeedX FNB OS, including FeedX Marketing Hub and the FeedX Marketing App used for Meta integrations. This policy explains personal-data processing through the FeedX website and platform, including staff administration and authorized business and customer-engagement workflows.

For processing whose purposes we determine, we act as the data controller. Where a business uses FeedX to manage its employees, customers or operations and determines the purposes of that processing, that business may be the data controller and we process its data on its instructions. The relevant employer or brand remains responsible for its own notices and lawful processing. A FeedX organization, brand or outlet is not necessarily a separate legal entity.

2. Information collected and its sources

The information processed depends on the features a business enables and your relationship with that business. Sources include you, authorized employers and staff, business records, connected platforms and service-generated activity.

  • Business users: names, contact and login information, employee/account links, organization, brand and outlet assignments, roles, permissions and activity records.
  • Employees and applicants: identity and contact details, employment and recruitment information, employer and workplace assignments, contracts, pay and bank details, rosters, attendance, leave, learning, performance and related operational records. Uploaded documents may contain additional personal or health information. Where an outlet enables attendance location verification, clock-in/out location and verification evidence are processed for attendance; this is not continuous location tracking.
  • Customers and other contacts: information you send to a business, such as your name or social identity, message and comment text, attachments, preferences, feedback and details needed to handle your inquiry. Customer, consent or redemption information is processed only where a corresponding business workflow is available and authorized.
  • Connected accounts: authorized Page/professional-account and business identifiers, granted permissions, assignment evidence, credential expiry, posts, media, publishing receipts, available engagement statistics, conversations and delivery/read events where authorized. Access tokens and related credentials are stored server-side.
  • Service operation: request, security, error and audit records; connection and webhook events; AI requests, outputs and usage; and technical information such as IP addresses and browser/device information available to our hosting and service providers.

Social identities are distinct from verified FeedX customer identities. A social account or message alone is not evidence that it belongs to an existing customer record. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information, passwords or payment credentials through customer messages.

3. Why information is processed

FeedX processes information to provide access and scoped workspaces; administer authorized employment and business operations; manage content, approvals and scheduled publication; receive, assign and respond to inquiries; maintain message and action history; measure available campaign and social results; generate permitted AI assistance; support users; prevent duplicate execution; secure integrations; investigate errors; and maintain evidence required for operational, contractual or legal purposes.

Information needed for account access, employment administration or a requested service may be required. Without it, the relevant function or inquiry may not be completed. Optional marketing consent and optional customer details are separate from information needed to deliver a requested service. This notice does not itself provide blanket consent.

4. Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp

Authorized business representatives connect Facebook Pages and linked Instagram Professional Accounts through official Meta authorization. FeedX uses granted access for the enabled functions: account discovery and verification, social publishing, post/insight synchronization and supported customer conversations or comments. Publishing, messaging and advertising have separate permissions and execution controls; connecting an account does not enable every function.

Messages and comments received through an authorized integration may be stored with their channel, account, sender identity, timestamps, thread history, assignments, notes, approved replies and delivery status so authorized staff can handle the conversation. Published content is transmitted to Meta and may be public according to the destination account. Meta independently processes information under its own terms and privacy policies.

WhatsApp integration, broader CRM audiences and marketing automation are future capabilities rather than currently verified live services. If introduced, only authorized and enabled functions will process the relevant business identifiers, phone numbers, messages, templates, consent/suppression evidence and delivery outcomes. Applicable opt-in, opt-out and platform messaging rules must be addressed before activation. This policy does not activate those services.

5. AI-assisted processing

Where a business enables AI assistance, relevant conversation context and approved Brand Knowledge may be sent to a third-party AI provider to suggest replies, summarize conversations or propose FAQ improvements. The current Marketing adapter uses OpenAI server-side. It limits history and removes recognizable email/phone patterns, but this is not complete anonymization: message text can still contain personal information.

Marketing currently uses Suggest mode: staff review and approve replies before sending. Unknown facts and complaints, refunds, allergens, food-safety or sensitive requests require human handling. AI output can be inaccurate and is not a reservation, price, availability or financial commitment. Employee or customer records are not generally accessible to Marketing AI simply because they exist elsewhere in FeedX.

The current reply adapter requests that OpenAI not store response application state. This does not establish zero retention; provider security and abuse-monitoring retention may still apply. Provider terms and account settings govern their processing. New AI providers or materially different uses require review and appropriate disclosure before activation.

6. Access, sharing and service providers

Information is available to authorized staff according to applicable role, organization, brand and outlet scope. It may be disclosed to the business responsible for the relevant employment or customer workflow; hosting, database, storage, authentication and AI providers; connected platforms to perform authorized actions; and advisers or authorities where necessary for lawful requests, obligations or disputes.

The current core infrastructure uses Supabase for platform data, authentication, storage and server functions; Vercel for website/application hosting; Meta for authorized social integrations; and OpenAI for configured AI processing. These providers process relevant information under their service arrangements and policies. Additional feature-specific providers require assessment before use. FeedX does not require transferring private employee records to Meta to publish social content or answer customer messages.

7. International processing

Our service providers and connected platforms may process or store information outside Malaysia. Applicable destinations and transfer arrangements depend on the configured service and provider. Transfers must be assessed against Malaysian PDPA requirements, including applicable conditions and safeguards. We do not represent that all information remains in Malaysia or that every provider has an approved zero-retention arrangement. Contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your data.

8. Security and browser storage

FeedX uses authenticated access, role and scope restrictions, server-side validation and protected data/storage authorities. Meta credentials are encrypted server-side; webhook processing verifies platform signatures. These controls reduce risk but do not guarantee that every security incident can be prevented. Applicable breach-notification obligations require assessment and action when an incident occurs.

Authenticated features use session-related browser storage and preferences such as theme or language. Hosting providers may process technical access logs. This privacy page requires no login and includes no analytics scripts, tracking pixels or social widgets. Connected websites and platforms have their own browser-storage practices.

9. Retention, disconnection and deletion

Different records serve different purposes. Employment, payroll, financial and audit evidence may require retention for applicable legal obligations or disputes. Conversations, social projections, media, AI outputs and technical records depend on their operational purpose and provider arrangements. We have not established one verified retention period covering all FeedX records and do not promise automatic deletion after a fixed number of days.

An authorized administrator can disconnect a Meta connection in Marketing Settings to stop its execution. A verified Meta revocation or data-deletion callback removes the affected authorization credentials and synchronized social-post records, and erases associated operational Inbox conversations, messages, notes, replies, AI summaries and suggestions, derived FAQs and webhook bodies within the affected authorization. A later authorization and unrelated records are kept separate. FeedX-owned creative revisions are not automatically deleted. Disconnection alone does not delete every FeedX record or content already published on Meta.

A verified customer request can also erase the specific external conversation and its derived records after identity and business scope are checked. An administrator's Meta identity is not treated as the identity of everyone messaging that account. Documented legal holds are restricted outside the operational Inbox and reviewed for release; ordinary operational history is not a reason to refuse a valid request. Minimal request, opt-out and replay evidence, and AI usage totals without conversation identifiers, may remain. The request outcome distinguishes local erasure, legally retained records and outstanding backup or provider handling; it does not claim immediate deletion of all third-party copies. Backups may expire under their applicable arrangements and must be checked when restoring erased data.

To request deletion of your personal data, email theyadvisory@gmail.com with the subject FeedX data deletion request, identify the relevant business/brand and account or conversation, and describe the data concerned. Do not send passwords, tokens or identity documents unless specifically requested through an appropriate channel. We may request proportionate identity or authority verification and coordinate with the responsible business. Applicable legal retention, third-party copies and backup arrangements can affect what can be deleted and when; the outcome and any restrictions should be explained.

You may also revoke the FeedX Marketing App through Meta's business-integration settings or use Meta's available data-deletion request flow. Content held directly by Meta is subject to Meta's separate controls. Contact FeedX for records that fall outside the callback's scope.

10. Marketing choices and privacy rights

Marketing consent must be specific to the relevant activity and recorded independently of this policy. You can withdraw optional marketing consent or request suppression by contacting the relevant brand or our privacy contact. An unsubscribe/opt-out request in a supported conversation stops automated eligibility for that conversation; it does not automatically erase records needed to respect the opt-out or handle a service request.

Subject to the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709), as amended, and applicable exceptions, you may request access to or correction of your personal data, withdraw consent, limit processing where applicable, object to direct marketing, and request data portability where technically feasible and compatible. You can request deletion and an explanation of any data we are required to retain. Identity checks, legally permitted fees or restrictions may apply; any applicable requirements should be explained when handling your request. You may raise a complaint with the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

11. Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, access/correction, consent withdrawal or deletion, contact The Y Advisory Sdn Bhd at theyadvisory@gmail.com or write to 19A, Jalan Bunga Raya, Taman Mayfair, 31650 Ipoh, Perak, Malaysia.

No Data Protection Officer has been formally appointed. The privacy contact is an inquiry channel and is not represented as a registered DPO. Applicable DPO appointment and data-controller registration requirements are being assessed separately.

When approved and published, this policy will be available at https://feedx.my/privacy. Changes will identify their effective date. A policy update alone does not authorize a new use that requires separate consent or approval.

Rekod mempunyai tujuan berbeza. Bukti pekerjaan, gaji, kewangan dan audit mungkin perlu disimpan untuk undang-undang atau pertikaian. Pemutusan sambungan tidak memadam semua rekod FeedX atau kandungan yang telah diterbitkan di Meta.

Hasil permintaan membezakan pemadaman setempat, rekod yang dikekalkan atas sebab undang-undang dan urusan sandaran atau penyedia yang belum selesai; ia tidak mendakwa semua salinan pihak ketiga dipadam serta-merta.
